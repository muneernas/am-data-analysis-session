From Numbers to Narratives

Participant handout · Data analysis & Power BI lab

Dataset: ahliyyah-mutran-learning.csv  ·  Time: ~50 minutes hands-on

Analysis engine (keep visible)

Look → Find patterns → Ask why → Take action

Insight formula

Who + What changed + By how much + So what

Diagnostic matrix (Ask why)

  • Instructional — Was teaching misaligned with the skill? → Adjust strategy / medium
  • Design — Was the task or rubric unclear? → Clarify assessment
  • Readiness — Missing prerequisites? → Scaffold gaps

Quick start in Power BI

  1. Open Power BI Desktop → Get data → Text/CSV → select the file → Load.
  2. Confirm Score, AttendancePct, and HomeworkCompletionPct are numbers (Column tools → Data type).
  3. Drag fields onto the canvas. Add Slicers for Term, GradeLevel, Subject.

Excel fallback: Insert → PivotChart / Charts. Same challenges apply.

Challenge 1 · Foundations (~12 min)

Build

Average Score by Subject — with a Term slicer

  • Which subject looks strongest? Weakest?
  • What changes when you switch Term 1 → Term 2?
  • Look beyond the average: any surprising highs or lows?

Challenge 2 · Relationships (~12 min)

Build

Scatter: AttendancePct (X) vs Score (Y)

  • Do you see a relationship?
  • Circle one outlier on your screen — what’s interesting about them?

Challenge 3 · Impact (~15 min)

Build

Term 1 vs Term 2 growth by LearningSupport

  • Compare Math Intervention / Reading Support vs None.
  • Diagnose: instructional, design, or readiness?
  • Choose a move: reteach / differentiate / adjust
